# Data and safety concerns

Do not put company schema snapshots, credentials, user records, account state, or raw private reports in public issues or this repository. Report a reproducible issue with synthetic inputs and the affected kit version. A maintainer should provide an appropriate private reporting route before requesting sensitive details.

The examples demonstrate scoped controls. They do not replace operating-system isolation, service permissions, company AI policy, or human authorization. If a control can be bypassed, preserve a safe local reproduction and identify its boundary. Disable the affected capability in the adopting project while its owner assesses it; do not broaden credentials to bypass a broken guard.
